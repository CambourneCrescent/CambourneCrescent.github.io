# Venue Management and Cleanliness

## **Room Bookings**
Room bookings will be managed carefully to avoid conflicts. A weekly review of room usage schedules will be conducted.

## **Cleanliness Standards**
Classrooms will be cleaned regularly, with assigned volunteers ensuring the standards are met. A cleanliness checklist will be provided for each room.

## **Annual Clean-up Day**
A community clean-up day will be organised annually, encouraging parents, students, and teachers to participate in maintaining the school environment.
