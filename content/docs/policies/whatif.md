# What If? 
## Scenarios Guide for Staff and Volunteers

### **1\. Teacher Absence**

* **Scenario**: A teacher is absent without prior notice.  
  * **Action Plan**:  
    * **Immediate Communication**: The absent teacher must inform the school coordinator as soon as possible via phone or WhatsApp.  
    * **Cover Arrangements**: The school coordinator will assign a backup teacher or a qualified volunteer to cover the class. In the absence of a substitute, a senior volunteer or class assistant should follow the pre-prepared lesson plan.  
    * **Lesson Plan Availability**: Teachers should submit their weekly lesson plans in advance, stored digitally (Google Docs or shared folder) for easy access by substitutes.  
    * **Notification to Parents**: If the absence will significantly impact the class, parents should be notified via the school’s WhatsApp group or email.

### **2\. Student behaviour Issues**

* **Scenario**: A student is disruptive during class (e.g., not listening, distracting others, showing defiant behaviour).  
  * **Action Plan**:  
    * **Verbal Warning**: The teacher gives a calm but firm verbal warning, explaining the disruptive behaviour and its impact on the class.  
    * **Time-Out**: If the behaviour continues, the student will be given a time-out in a designated quiet area for reflection (no more than 5 minutes).  
    * **Discussion**: After class, the teacher will have a one-on-one conversation with the student to understand the underlying cause of the behaviour and to reinforce expected conduct.  
    * **Parental Involvement**: If the behaviour persists across multiple sessions, the teacher will inform the parents and discuss possible solutions (e.g., positive reinforcement at home, additional support).  
    * **Referral to Coordinator**: For severe cases, the issue will be escalated to the school coordinator, who will schedule a meeting with the parents.

### **3\. A Volunteer is Late or Absent**

* **Scenario**: A volunteer responsible for helping with classroom activities or operations is late or absent.  
  * **Action Plan**:  
    * **Immediate Communication**: Volunteers must inform the school coordinator or class teacher if they anticipate being late or absent.  
    * **Task Redistribution**: The teacher or coordinator will reassign tasks to available volunteers or staff for that session. This could include dividing responsibilities (e.g., managing snack time, organizing materials).  
    * **Volunteer Roster**: Maintain a weekly volunteer roster with backup personnel for critical tasks like classroom support and monitoring student breaks.

### **4\. Classroom Materials or Resources Are Missing**

* **Scenario**: Key teaching materials or resources (e.g., textbooks, whiteboard markers) are missing or unavailable.  
  * **Action Plan**:  
    * **Use Digital Resources**: Teachers can switch to digital tools (e.g., tablets, phones) to access materials like Qur'an verses, Arabic worksheets, or educational videos.  
    * **Improvise**: Teachers can adapt the lesson by encouraging group discussions, storytelling, or using available materials creatively (e.g., using a chalkboard if whiteboard markers are missing).  
    * **Restock Procedure**: Ensure that a stock of essential materials is always available, and teachers are required to inform the coordinator about low stock at least a week in advance.

### **5\. A Student Falls Ill During Class**

* **Scenario**: A student becomes unwell or has a medical emergency during school hours.  
  * **Action Plan**:  
    * **Immediate Care**: The teacher or volunteer provides immediate care, ensuring the student is taken to a quiet, comfortable area.  
    * **First-Aid Response**: If first-aid is required, the designated first-aider on duty will administer necessary care.  
    * **Notify Parents**: The school coordinator must immediately notify the parents, requesting them to pick up their child if needed.  
    * **Emergency Services**: In the case of a severe illness or injury, call 999 for emergency medical assistance.  
    * **Incident Report**: Complete a written incident report to log the situation and actions taken.

### **6\. A Parent is Late for Pickup**

* **Scenario**: A parent is late in picking up their child after school hours.  
  * **Action Plan**:  
    * **Supervision**: The child will remain supervised by a designated teacher or volunteer in a safe space, preferably in the main hall or a designated waiting area.  
    * **Parent Communication**: Attempt to contact the parent via phone, using the emergency contact details provided during registration.  
    * **Escalation**: If the parent is unreachable after 30 minutes, escalate the matter to the school coordinator, who may need to contact an alternative emergency contact or, in rare cases, local authorities.

### **7\. A Student is Absent Without Prior Notice**

* **Scenario**: A student is absent without prior notification from parents.  
  * **Action Plan**:  
    * **Contact the Parents**: The coordinator or designated attendance officer should contact the parents after the first hour of class to check on the student’s well-being.  
    * **Record Absences**: Record the absence in the attendance register and, if frequent, follow up with the parents to address potential issues such as health concerns or lack of engagement.  
    * **Encouraging Attendance**: Discuss strategies with parents for improving attendance if necessary, such as incentives or personalised support.

### **8\. Teacher or Student Conflict**

* **Scenario**: A conflict arises between a teacher and a student during class (e.g., the student feels unfairly treated or the teacher feels disrespected).  
  * **Action Plan**:  
    * **Calm Intervention**: The teacher will address the issue calmly and privately with the student after class to understand both perspectives.  
    * **Involve the Coordinator**: If the issue is not resolved, the teacher will involve the school coordinator to mediate a discussion between the teacher, student, and parents if necessary.  
    * **Documentation**: The conflict and any resolutions will be documented for future reference in case of repeated incidents.

### **9\. Parent Complaint**

* **Scenario**: A parent raises a concern or complaint about the school (e.g., teaching methods, classroom behaviour management).  
  * **Action Plan**:  
    * **Acknowledge the Complaint**: Listen to the parent’s concerns respectfully and thank them for raising the issue.  
    * **Immediate Follow-Up**: Inform the school coordinator of the complaint and schedule a meeting with the parent to discuss the matter in detail.  
    * **Action Plan**: Based on the outcome of the meeting, create an action plan to address the issue and follow up with the parent to ensure satisfaction.  
    * **Record the Complaint**: Document the complaint and resolution steps for transparency and tracking.

### **10\. Facility Issue (e.g., Heating, Lighting, or Cleanliness)**

* **Scenario**: There is an issue with the facility, such as no heating, broken lighting, or unclean classrooms.  
  * **Action Plan**:  
    * **Immediate Fix**: Notify the venue manager or on-site staff to resolve the issue as quickly as possible.  
    * **Temporary Solutions**: If the issue cannot be fixed immediately, relocate the class to a different room or space. For cleanliness issues, assign available volunteers to clean up.  
    * **Long-Term Resolution**: Document the issue and schedule a meeting with the venue staff to ensure long-term solutions, especially for recurring problems.
