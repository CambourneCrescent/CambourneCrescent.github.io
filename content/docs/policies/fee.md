# Fees Policy

All parents and guardians are responsible for ensuring that fees are paid on time via **monthly standing order for 12 months**, covering school operations, books, and other costs.

Crescent School encourages all students to attend the **full four hours of classes**, which cover **Qur’an, Islamic Studies, and Arabic Language**.  

- The **same fee applies** whether a student attends **2 hours** or **4 hours**.  
- Fees cover **12 months**, including the additional cost of books.  
- In cases of **late payment**, parents will be requested to pay the **full yearly fee** to reduce administration time spent on sending reminders.  

---
## Fee Structure

Please note that the school fees have increased by **£5 per category per month**.  

- **One child**: £45 per month
- **Two children**: £80 per month
- **Three or more children**: £95 per month
