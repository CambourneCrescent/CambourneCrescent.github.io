We have thoughtfully curated a mix of **compulsory** and **optional** lessons, aiming to cater to the diverse interests and preferences of our students.

## Compulsory Lessons

- **Qur'an Classes**  
  These sessions will cover learning the Arabic alphabet and progress to reading the Qur'an with proper *Tajwīd* and pronunciation.

- **Islamic Studies**  
  Building on the Safar books, these classes will provide valuable insights into Islamic teachings.

- **Arabic Language Class**  
  Similar to the existing curriculum, this class will continue to teach the Arabic language in a structured manner.

## Adults' Arabic Class

  We are pleased to offer Arabic language classes for interested adults.
