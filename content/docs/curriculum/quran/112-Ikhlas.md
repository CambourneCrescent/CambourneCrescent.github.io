# 🕋 Qur’an Syllabus: **Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ**  

---

## 📖 Surah Overview

- **Name:** Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ (The Sincerity)
- **Position in Qur’an:** 112th Surah  
- **Verses:** 4 (Āyāt)  
- **Revelation:** Makkiyyah

---

## 📜 Surah Ikhlas

> **بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ**  
> قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ  
> ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ  
> لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ  
> وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Recite Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ fluently with correct Tajwīd.
- Understand the meaning and significance of the surah.
- Memorise the surah with correct pronunciation.
- Appreciate the concept of **Tawḥīd** (Oneness of Allāh).

---

## 🗣️ Tajwīd Focus

- Correct **Makharij** (pronunciation of letters).
- Introduction to **Madd** (elongation).
- Awareness of rules for **Lām** and **Llāh** in the name "اللَّهُ".

---

## 🧠 Memorisation Strategy

- Memorise 1 āyah at a time using repetition and teacher guidance.
- Practice with peers and at home for reinforcement.
- Weekly revision in class and at home.

---

## 🗒️ Key Vocabulary (Optional)

| Arabic Word | Meaning         |
|-------------|-----------------|
| أَحَدٌ       | One / Unique    |
| ٱلصَّمَدُ     | The Eternal     |
| لَمْ يَلِدْ   | He begets not   |
| كُفُوًا      | Equal / Comparable |

---

## 💬 Reflection and Application

- Discuss what it means that Allāh is **One** and **Eternal**.
- Reflect on how belief in the Oneness of Allāh shapes a Muslim's life.
- Encourage children to say this surah in daily prayers.
