# 🕋 Qur’an Syllabus: **Sūrah al-Falaq**

---

## 📖 Surah Overview

- **Name:** Sūrah al-Falaq (The Daybreak)  
- **Position in Qur’an:** 113th Surah  
- **Verses:** 5 (Āyāt)  
- **Revelation:** Makkiyyah

---

## 📜 Surah Al-Falaq

> **بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ**  
> **قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ**  
> **مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ**  
> **وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ**  
> **وَمِن شَرِّ ٱلنَّفَّٰثَٰتِ فِي ٱلْعُقَدِ**  
> **وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ**

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Recite Sūrah al-Falaq fluently with correct Tajwīd.  
- Understand the meaning and significance of seeking refuge in Allāh from all evil.  
- Memorise the surah with correct pronunciation.  
- Appreciate the concept of protection and reliance on Allāh.

---

## 🗣️ Tajwīd Focus

- Proper articulation of **Qalqalah** letters.  
- Awareness of **Ghunna** (nasalisation) in **ʿAʿūdhu** and **Nafāthāt**.  
- Correct elongation (Madd) in specific words.

---

## 🧠 Memorisation Strategy

- Memorise 1 āyah at a time with repetition.  
- Practice regularly at home and in class.  
- Review in groups for reinforcement.

---

## 🗒️ Key Vocabulary (Optional)

| Arabic Word      | Meaning                |
|------------------|------------------------|
| أَعُوذُ          | I seek refuge          |
| رَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ  | Lord of the daybreak   |
| شَرِّ             | Evil                   |
| خَلَقَ            | Created                |
| غَاسِقٍ           | Darkness (night)       |
| نَفَّٰثَٰتِ       | Those who blow (witches) |
| عُقَدِ            | Knots                  |
| حَاسِدٍ           | Envier                 |

---

## 💬 Reflection and Application

- Reflect on the importance of seeking protection from harm and evil.  
- Encourage daily recitation as a means of spiritual protection.  
- Discuss how turning to Allāh can bring peace and safety in difficult situations.
