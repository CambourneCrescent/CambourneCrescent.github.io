# 🕋 Qur’an Syllabus: **Sūrah aṭ-Ṭāriq**

---

## 📖 Surah Overview

- **Name:** Sūrah aṭ-Ṭāriq (The Morning Star)  
- **Position in Qur’an:** 86th Surah  
- **Verses:** 17 (Āyāt)  
- **Revelation:** Makkiyyah  

---

## 📜 Sūrah aṭ-Ṭāriq

> **بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ**  
> وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلطَّارِقِ  
> وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلطَّارِقُ  
> ٱلنَّجْمُ ٱلثَّاقِبُ  
> إِن كُلُّ نَفْسٍۢ لَّمَّا عَلَيْهَا حَافِظٌۭ  
> فَلْيَنظُرِ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنُ مِمَّ خُلِقَ  
> خُلِقَ مِن مَّآءٍۢ دَافِقٍۢ  
> يَخْرُجُ مِنۢ بَيْنِ ٱلصُّلْبِ وَٱلتَّرَآئِبِ  
> إِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ رَجْعِهِۦ لَقَادِرٌۭ  
> يَوْمَ تُبْلَى ٱلسَّرَآئِرُ  
> فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن قُوَّةٍۢ وَلَا نَاصِرٍۢ  
> وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلرَّجْعِ  
> وَٱلْأَرْضِ ذَاتِ ٱلصَّدْعِ  
> إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوْلٌۭ فَصْلٌۭ  
> وَمَا هُوَ بِٱلْهَزْلِ  
> إِنَّهُمْ يَكِيدُونَ كَيْدًۭا  
> وَأَكِيدُ كَيْدًۭا  
> فَمَهِّلِ ٱلْكَـٰفِرِينَ أَمْهِلْهُمْ رُوَيْدًۭا  

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

- Recite Sūrah aṭ-Ṭāriq fluently with correct Tajwīd.  
- Understand the concept of divine protection and accountability.  
- Memorise the surah through guided practice and repetition.  
- Develop a deeper appreciation for the signs of Allāh in the heavens and within the human being.

---

## 🗣️ Tajwīd Focus

- Practice proper **Qalqalah** in words like **الطارق** and **الصّلب**.  
- Apply **Ghunna** (nasalisation) where relevant.  
- Ensure clear articulation of long vowels and **Madd**.

---

## 🧠 Memorisation Strategy

- Break the surah into 4–5 sections for manageable learning.  
- Use teacher-led recitation with echoing for reinforcement.  
- Regular peer practice and home assignments for consistency.

---

## 💬 Reflection and Application

- Reflect on how every soul has a guardian and will be held accountable.  
- Discuss how this surah affirms Allāh’s power over creation and resurrection.  
- Encourage children to look at nature and life as signs of Allāh’s power.
