    Parents

    Parents, Fees, Attendance, Drop-Off, Collection, Policy, School Rules, Communication

    📆 Attendance and Absence Policy #

    ⏰ Operating Hours #

    • The school operates on Sundays from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM during term time.
    • During Ramadan, the school runs on a reduced timetable, typically until 1:00 PM.

    Break Times #

    Children are given a 5 to 10-minute break after every hour. Please note that it is not possible to take children outside to the school grounds during breaks, as we do not have access to the grounds.
