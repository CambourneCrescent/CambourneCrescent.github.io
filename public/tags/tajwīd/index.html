    112-Al-Ikhlāṣ

    June 23, 2025
    Education, Islamic School
    Qur’an, Memorisation, Tajwīd, Class 1

    🕋 Qur’an Syllabus: Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ #

    📖 Surah Overview #

    • Name: Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ (The Sincerity)
    • Position in Qur’an: 112th Surah
    • Verses: 4 (Āyāt)
    • Revelation: Makkiyyah

    📜 Surah Ikhlas #

    بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
    قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
    ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ
    لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
    وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ

    🎯 Learning Objectives #

    • Recite Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ fluently with correct Tajwīd.
    • Understand the meaning and significance of the surah.
    • Memorise the surah with correct pronunciation.
    • Appreciate the concept of Tawḥīd (Oneness of Allāh).

    🗣️ Tajwīd Focus #

    • Correct Makharij (pronunciation of letters).
    • Introduction to Madd (elongation).
    • Awareness of rules for Lām and Llāh in the name “اللَّهُ”.

    🧠 Memorisation Strategy #

    • Memorise 1 āyah at a time using repetition and teacher guidance.
    • Practice with peers and at home for reinforcement.
    • Weekly revision in class and at home.

    🗒️ Key Vocabulary (Optional) #

    Arabic Word Meaning
    أَحَدٌ One / Unique
    ٱلصَّمَدُ The Eternal
    لَمْ يَلِدْ He begets not
    كُفُوًا Equal / Comparable

    💬 Reflection and Application #

    • Discuss what it means that Allāh is One and Eternal.
    • Reflect on how belief in the Oneness of Allāh shapes a Muslim’s life.
    • Encourage children to say this surah in daily prayers.

    113-Al-Falaq

    June 23, 2025
    Education, Islamic School
    Qur’an, Memorisation, Tajwīd, Class 1

    🕋 Qur’an Syllabus: Sūrah al-Falaq #

    📖 Surah Overview #

    • Name: Sūrah al-Falaq (The Daybreak)
    • Position in Qur’an: 113th Surah
    • Verses: 5 (Āyāt)
    • Revelation: Makkiyyah

    📜 Surah Al-Falaq #

    بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
    قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ
    مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
    وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ
    وَمِن شَرِّ ٱلنَّفَّٰثَٰتِ فِي ٱلْعُقَدِ
    وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ

    🎯 Learning Objectives #

    • Recite Sūrah al-Falaq fluently with correct Tajwīd.
    • Understand the meaning and significance of seeking refuge in Allāh from all evil.
    • Memorise the surah with correct pronunciation.
    • Appreciate the concept of protection and reliance on Allāh.

    🗣️ Tajwīd Focus #

    • Proper articulation of Qalqalah letters.
    • Awareness of Ghunna (nasalisation) in ʿAʿūdhu and Nafāthāt.
    • Correct elongation (Madd) in specific words.

    🧠 Memorisation Strategy #

    • Memorise 1 āyah at a time with repetition.
    • Practice regularly at home and in class.
    • Review in groups for reinforcement.

    🗒️ Key Vocabulary (Optional) #

    Arabic Word Meaning
    أَعُوذُ I seek refuge
    رَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ Lord of the daybreak
    شَرِّ Evil
    خَلَقَ Created
    غَاسِقٍ Darkness (night)
    نَفَّٰثَٰتِ Those who blow (witches)
    عُقَدِ Knots
    حَاسِدٍ Envier

    💬 Reflection and Application #

    • Reflect on the importance of seeking protection from harm and evil.
    • Encourage daily recitation as a means of spiritual protection.
    • Discuss how turning to Allāh can bring peace and safety in difficult situations.
