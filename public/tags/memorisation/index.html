    1-Al-Fātiḥah

    Education, Islamic School, Qur’an
    Qur’an, Memorisation, Surah, Tajwid

    🕋 Qur’an Syllabus: Sūrah al-Fātiḥah #

    📖 Surah Overview #

    • Name: Sūrah al-Fātiḥah (The Opening)
    • Position in Qur’an: 1st Surah
    • Verses: 7 (Āyāt)
    • Revelation: Makkiyyah

    📜 Surah Fatiha #

    بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
    ٱلْـحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ
    ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
    مَـٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ
    إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
    ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ
    صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

    🌟 Learning Objectives #

    • Memorise the Sūrah with correct Tajwīd
    • Understand the meaning of key words and phrases
    • Reflect on the central themes of guidance, mercy, and worship

    📝 Memorisation Strategy #

    • Repeat each verse multiple times
    • Recite with a teacher or parent to ensure correct pronunciation
    • Listen to recitation by qualified Qāriʾ
    • Practise regularly in small sections

    🧠 Key Vocabulary #

    • ٱلْـحَمْدُ – All praise
    • رَبِّ – Lord, Sustainer
    • ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ – The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful
    • مَـٰلِكِ – Master
    • ٱهْدِنَا – Guide us
    • ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ – The straight path

    💭 Reflection #

    Sūrah al-Fātiḥah is a powerful duʿā and the opening chapter of the Qur’an. It expresses our need for Allah’s guidance and affirms His mercy and lordship. It is recited in every unit of prayer (ṣalāh), making its memorisation and understanding essential for all Muslims.

    📖 Qur’an Curriculum Overview #

    Our Qur’an programme emphasises Tajwīd, reading fluency, memorisation, and the learning of essential duʿās. Students progress through a carefully structured syllabus from Class 1 to Class 7, allowing them to gradually develop confidence, skill, and a connection with the Qur’an.

    🧠 Memorisation Curriculum #

    Each class memorises a set of short Sūrahs from Juz ʿAmma, along with other important verses and Surahs, beginning with those that are easiest to learn. The memorisation schedule is spread across three terms each year. As students progress, they are gradually introduced to longer and more complex Sūrahs suited to their age and ability.

    Qur’an, Memorisation, Tajwīd, Class 4

    🕋 Qur’an Syllabus: Sūrah aṭ-Ṭāriq #

    📖 Surah Overview #

    • Name: Sūrah aṭ-Ṭāriq (The Morning Star)
    • Position in Qur’an: 86th Surah
    • Verses: 17 (Āyāt)
    • Revelation: Makkiyyah

    📜 Sūrah aṭ-Ṭāriq #

    بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
    وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلطَّارِقِ
    وَمَآ أَدْرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلطَّارِقُ
    ٱلنَّجْمُ ٱلثَّاقِبُ
    إِن كُلُّ نَفْسٍۢ لَّمَّا عَلَيْهَا حَافِظٌۭ
    فَلْيَنظُرِ ٱلْإِنسَـٰنُ مِمَّ خُلِقَ
    خُلِقَ مِن مَّآءٍۢ دَافِقٍۢ
    يَخْرُجُ مِنۢ بَيْنِ ٱلصُّلْبِ وَٱلتَّرَآئِبِ
    إِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ رَجْعِهِۦ لَقَادِرٌۭ
    يَوْمَ تُبْلَى ٱلسَّرَآئِرُ
    فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن قُوَّةٍۢ وَلَا نَاصِرٍۢ
    وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلرَّجْعِ
    وَٱلْأَرْضِ ذَاتِ ٱلصَّدْعِ
    إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوْلٌۭ فَصْلٌۭ
    وَمَا هُوَ بِٱلْهَزْلِ
    إِنَّهُمْ يَكِيدُونَ كَيْدًۭا
    وَأَكِيدُ كَيْدًۭا
    فَمَهِّلِ ٱلْكَـٰفِرِينَ أَمْهِلْهُمْ رُوَيْدًۭا

    112-Al-Ikhlāṣ

    June 23, 2025
    Education, Islamic School
    Qur’an, Memorisation, Tajwīd, Class 1

    🕋 Qur’an Syllabus: Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ #

    📖 Surah Overview #

    • Name: Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ (The Sincerity)
    • Position in Qur’an: 112th Surah
    • Verses: 4 (Āyāt)
    • Revelation: Makkiyyah

    📜 Surah Ikhlas #

    No. Ayah
    1 قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
    2 ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ
    3 لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
    4 وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ

    🎯 Learning Objectives #

    • Recite Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ fluently with correct Tajwīd.
    • Understand the meaning and significance of the surah.
    • Memorise the surah with correct pronunciation.
    • Appreciate the concept of Tawḥīd (Oneness of Allāh).

    🗣️ Tajwīd Focus #

    • Correct Makharij (pronunciation of letters).
    • Introduction to Madd (elongation).
    • Awareness of rules for Lām and Llāh in the name “اللَّهُ”.

    🧠 Memorisation Strategy #

    • Memorise 1 āyah at a time using repetition and teacher guidance.
    • Practice with peers and at home for reinforcement.
    • Weekly revision in class and at home.

    🗒️ Key Vocabulary (Optional) #

    Arabic Word Meaning
    أَحَدٌ One / Unique
    ٱلصَّمَدُ The Eternal
    لَمْ يَلِدْ He begets not
    كُفُوًا Equal / Comparable

    💬 Reflection and Application #

    • Discuss what it means that Allāh is One and Eternal.
    • Reflect on how belief in the Oneness of Allāh shapes a Muslim’s life.
    • Encourage children to say this surah in daily prayers.

    113-Al-Falaq

    June 23, 2025
    Education, Islamic School
    Qur’an, Memorisation, Tajwīd, Class 1

    🕋 Qur’an Syllabus: Sūrah al-Falaq #

    📖 Surah Overview #

    • Name: Sūrah al-Falaq (The Daybreak)
    • Position in Qur’an: 113th Surah
    • Verses: 5 (Āyāt)
    • Revelation: Makkiyyah

    📜 Surah Al-Falaq #

    بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
    قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ
    مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
    وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ
    وَمِن شَرِّ ٱلنَّفَّٰثَٰتِ فِي ٱلْعُقَدِ
    وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ

    🎯 Learning Objectives #

    • Recite Sūrah al-Falaq fluently with correct Tajwīd.
    • Understand the meaning and significance of seeking refuge in Allāh from all evil.
    • Memorise the surah with correct pronunciation.
    • Appreciate the concept of protection and reliance on Allāh.

    🗣️ Tajwīd Focus #

    • Proper articulation of Qalqalah letters.
    • Awareness of Ghunna (nasalisation) in ʿAʿūdhu and Nafāthāt.
    • Correct elongation (Madd) in specific words.

    🧠 Memorisation Strategy #

    • Memorise 1 āyah at a time with repetition.
    • Practice regularly at home and in class.
    • Review in groups for reinforcement.

    🗒️ Key Vocabulary (Optional) #

    Arabic Word Meaning
    أَعُوذُ I seek refuge
    رَبِّ ٱلْفَلَقِ Lord of the daybreak
    شَرِّ Evil
    خَلَقَ Created
    غَاسِقٍ Darkness (night)
    نَفَّٰثَٰتِ Those who blow (witches)
    عُقَدِ Knots
    حَاسِدٍ Envier

    💬 Reflection and Application #

    • Reflect on the importance of seeking protection from harm and evil.
    • Encourage daily recitation as a means of spiritual protection.
    • Discuss how turning to Allāh can bring peace and safety in difficult situations.
