    1-Al-Fātiḥah

    Education, Islamic School, Qur’an
    Qur’an, Memorisation, Surah, Tajwid

    🕋 Qur’an Syllabus: Sūrah al-Fātiḥah #

    📖 Surah Overview #

    • Name: Sūrah al-Fātiḥah (The Opening)
    • Position in Qur’an: 1st Surah
    • Verses: 7 (Āyāt)
    • Revelation: Makkiyyah

    📜 Surah Fatiha #

    بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
    ٱلْـحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلْعَـٰلَمِينَ
    ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
    مَـٰلِكِ يَوْمِ ٱلدِّينِ
    إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ
    ٱهْدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ
    صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ ٱلْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ

    🌟 Learning Objectives #

    • Memorise the Sūrah with correct Tajwīd
    • Understand the meaning of key words and phrases
    • Reflect on the central themes of guidance, mercy, and worship

    📝 Memorisation Strategy #

    • Repeat each verse multiple times
    • Recite with a teacher or parent to ensure correct pronunciation
    • Listen to recitation by qualified Qāriʾ
    • Practise regularly in small sections

    🧠 Key Vocabulary #

    • ٱلْـحَمْدُ – All praise
    • رَبِّ – Lord, Sustainer
    • ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ – The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful
    • مَـٰلِكِ – Master
    • ٱهْدِنَا – Guide us
    • ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلْمُسْتَقِيمَ – The straight path

    💭 Reflection #

    Sūrah al-Fātiḥah is a powerful duʿā and the opening chapter of the Qur’an. It expresses our need for Allah’s guidance and affirms His mercy and lordship. It is recited in every unit of prayer (ṣalāh), making its memorisation and understanding essential for all Muslims.
