        June 19, 2025
        Education, Islamic School
        Arabic, Kitabi Series, Language Curriculum

        Arabic language Curriculum Overview #

        Our Arabic language curriculum is based on the Kitābī Series, a progressive, child-friendly programme designed to build proficiency in reading, writing, listening, and speaking Arabic. The curriculum focuses on grammar (naḥw), vocabulary, sentence structure, and comprehension through engaging themes and structured progression.

        Objectives #

        • To introduce and reinforce Arabic reading and writing skills.
        • To build a strong vocabulary base through thematic learning.
        • To develop grammar understanding in context (naḥw and ṣarf).
        • To promote listening and speaking fluency.
        • To connect students to Qur’ānic and classical Arabic.

        Crescent School - Curriculum #

        At Crescent School, we have thoughtfully curated a balanced curriculum that supports both academic and spiritual growth. Our aim is to nurture young minds with strong Islamic values while fostering a love for learning.

        We offer a mix of compulsory and optional lessons designed to cater to the diverse interests and needs of our students.

        📘 Compulsory Lessons (Children) #

        • Qur’an Classes
          Pupils begin by learning the Arabic alphabet and basic phonics, gradually progressing to fluent recitation of the Qur’an with correct Tajwīd and pronunciation.

        June 19, 2025

        Islamic Studies Curriculum Overview #

        Our Islamic Studies curriculum is based on the Safar Islamic Studies series, a comprehensive and age-appropriate programme that builds Islamic knowledge step by step. The curriculum is structured to cater to different age groups and learning stages, blending theory with practical application.

        Objectives #

        • To instil core Islamic beliefs (ʿAqīdah) and values from an early age.
        • To develop love for Allah ﷻ and His Messenger ﷺ.
        • To provide children with a solid foundation in Islamic history, manners, and daily practices.
        • To nurture students’ spiritual, moral, and social development in line with the teachings of Islam.

        Sūrah An-Nabaʾ (An-Nabaʾ) #

        An-Nabaʾ #

        بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
        عَمَّ يَتَسَاءَلُونَ
        عَنِ النَّبَإِ الْعَظِيمِ
        الَّذِي هُمْ فِيهِ مُخْتَلِفُونَ
        كَلَّا سَيَعْلَمُونَ
        ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَيَعْلَمُونَ
        …

        🎯 Learning Objectives #

        • To memorise Sūrah An-Nabaʾ with correct pronunciation and fluency.
        • To understand the meaning and importance of the chapter in Islamic belief.
        • To apply basic tajwīd rules where applicable.

        🧠 Tajwīd Focus #

        • Identify and practise tajwīd rules in the surah such as Madd, Idghām, or Qalqalah.

        📝 Memorisation Strategy #

        • Break the surah into smaller parts for easier recall.
        • Repeat each line multiple times with a teacher or parent.
        • Listen to recitations to improve fluency and tajwīd.
        • Practise daily and check pronunciation accuracy.

        💭 Reflection #

        Sūrah An-Nabaʾ contains powerful messages that enhance our understanding of Allāh’s attributes and our responsibilities as Muslims.

        112-Al-Ikhlāṣ

        June 23, 2025
        Education, Islamic School
        Qur’an, Memorisation, Tajwīd, Class 1

        🕋 Qur’an Syllabus: Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ #

        Class 1 – Autumn Term

        📖 Surah Overview #

        • Name: Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ (The Sincerity)
        • Position in Qur’an: 112th Surah
        • Verses: 4 (Āyāt)
        • Revelation: Makkiyyah

        📜 Surah #

        بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ
        قُلْ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ
        ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ
        لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ
        وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ

        🎯 Learning Objectives #

        • Recite Sūrah al-Ikhlāṣ fluently with correct Tajwīd.
        • Understand the meaning and significance of the surah.
        • Memorise the surah with correct pronunciation.
        • Appreciate the concept of Tawḥīd (Oneness of Allāh).

        🗣️ Tajwīd Focus #

        • Correct Makharij (pronunciation of letters).
        • Introduction to Madd (elongation).
        • Awareness of rules for Lām and Llāh in the name “اللَّهُ”.

        🧠 Memorisation Strategy #

        • Memorise 1 āyah at a time using repetition and teacher guidance.
        • Practice with peers and at home for reinforcement.
        • Weekly revision in class and at home.

        🗒️ Key Vocabulary (Optional) #

        Arabic WordMeaning
        أَحَدٌOne / Unique
        ٱلصَّمَدُThe Eternal
        لَمْ يَلِدْHe begets not
        كُفُوًاEqual / Comparable

        💬 Reflection and Application #

        • Discuss what it means that Allāh is One and Eternal.
        • Reflect on how belief in the Oneness of Allāh shapes a Muslim’s life.
        • Encourage children to say this surah in daily prayers.
