    Crescent School #

    Welcome to Crescent School, a weekend Arabic Language and Islamic school dedicated to nurturing young hearts and minds with knowledge, character development, and community spirit.

    About the School #

    Crescent School was established in 2011 to meet the growing religious, educational, cultural, and recreational needs of the Cambourne community. Our aim is to provide children with a strong foundation in Islamic values and teachings, in a warm, inclusive, and engaging environment.

    We combine traditional education with a modern approach to teaching, ensuring students gain not only knowledge but also confidence, identity, and life skills.

    Admissions #

    Admissions are open to all members of the community. To enrol, children must be in Year 1 or above. New academic batches begin every September, and places are offered on a first-come, first-served basis.

    To apply, please complete the Student Application Form.

    School Days & Times #

    🕙 Sundays
    🗓 During school term time
    📍 Cambourne Village College
    🕒 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM

    Crescent School runs every Sunday during term time, with four hours of structured lessons including Qur’an, Islamic Studies, Arabic Language, and optional enrichment classes.

    Curriculum Overview #

    Our curriculum blends traditional and contemporary methods, tailored to suit each age group:

    • Qur’an: Reading with tajwīd, memorisation (ḥifẓ), and understanding
    • Islamic Studies: Based on the Safar syllabus – covering ʿaqīdah, sīrah, fiqh, and manners
    • Arabic Language: Language skills including reading, writing, speaking and comprehension
