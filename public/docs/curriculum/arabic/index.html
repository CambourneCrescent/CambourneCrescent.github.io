    Arabic language Curriculum Overview #

    Our Arabic language curriculum is based on the Kitābī Series, a progressive, child-friendly programme designed to build proficiency in reading, writing, listening, and speaking Arabic. The curriculum focuses on grammar (naḥw), vocabulary, sentence structure, and comprehension through engaging themes and structured progression.

    Objectives #

    • To introduce and reinforce Arabic reading and writing skills.
    • To build a strong vocabulary base through thematic learning.
    • To develop grammar understanding in context (naḥw and ṣarf).
    • To promote listening and speaking fluency.
    • To connect students to Qur’ānic and classical Arabic.

    Class Breakdown #

    Class 1 (Ages 5–6) #

    Based on: Kitābī 1

    • Arabic alphabet recognition and pronunciation
    • Tracing and writing letters in initial/medial/final positions
    • Basic vocabulary: colours, animals, body parts
    • Short phrases and greetings
    • Simple oral activities and songs

    Class 2 (Ages 6–7) #

    Based on: Kitābī 2

    • Revising letters and joining them to form words
    • Introduction to short vowels (fatḥah, kasrah, ḍammah)
    • Vocabulary: household items, food, family members
    • Introduction to demonstrative pronouns (هذا / هذه)
    • Listening and repeating short dialogues

    Class 3 (Ages 7–8) #

    Based on: Kitābī 3

    • Reading full sentences with vowels
    • Use of definite article (الـ)
    • Vocabulary: school, classroom, clothes
    • Introduction to simple verbs in past tense (3rd person)
    • Writing basic sentences using vocabulary and structure

    Class 4 (Ages 8–9) #

    Based on: Kitābī 4

    • Singular/plural, masculine/feminine forms
    • Verbs in past tense (he/she) and subject-verb agreement
    • Vocabulary: weather, time, days of the week
    • Building short paragraphs with meaning
    • Basic comprehension of stories

    Class 5 (Ages 9–10) #

    Based on: Kitābī 5

    • Introduction to present tense verbs
    • Using verbs with personal pronouns (أنا، أنتَ، هو، هي)
    • Reading longer texts and understanding main ideas
    • Vocabulary: travel, places, feelings
    • Writing structured responses to simple prompts

    Class 6 (Ages 10–11) #

    Based on: Kitābī 6

    • Regular and irregular verbs in past and present tenses
    • Simple conjunctions (و، ثم، لأنّ)
    • Describing people and places in full sentences
    • Reading short stories and answering questions
    • Oral storytelling and presentations

    Class GCSE (Ages 11–13) #

    Based on: Kitābī GCSE syllabus

    • Sentence structure: nominal vs verbal sentences
    • Understanding and using root letters (جذر) and verb forms
    • Descriptive writing and letter writing in Arabic
    • Comprehending and discussing longer passages
    • Introduction to basic Qur’ānic Arabic structures

    Assessment & Enrichment #

    • Weekly vocabulary quizzes and grammar checks
    • Termly speaking & listening assessments
    • Writing tasks focused on sentence and paragraph development
    • Arabic conversation activities to boost fluency
    • Linking Arabic to Qur’ānic phrases and duʿās

    Our approach is interactive, engaging, and paced to match the child’s developmental stage—ensuring a joyful and meaningful connection with the Arabic language.
