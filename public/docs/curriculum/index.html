    Crescent School - Curriculum #

    At Crescent School, we have thoughtfully curated a balanced curriculum that supports both academic and spiritual growth. Our aim is to nurture young minds with strong Islamic values while fostering a love for learning.

    We offer a mix of compulsory and optional lessons designed to cater to the diverse interests and needs of our students.

    📘 Compulsory Lessons (Children) #

    • Qur’an Classes
      Pupils begin by learning the Arabic alphabet and basic phonics, gradually progressing to fluent recitation of the Qur’an with correct Tajwīd and pronunciation.

    • Islamic Studies
      Using the Safar Publications series, students explore core Islamic beliefs, worship, manners, and the life of the Prophet ﷺ through age-appropriate, engaging content.

    • Arabic Language
      Our Arabic classes follow a structured curriculum aimed at building proficiency in reading, writing, speaking, and understanding the Arabic language.

    🧕 Adults’ Arabic Class #

    We are pleased to offer Arabic classes for adults in the community who wish to connect with the language of the Qur’an. These sessions are beginner-friendly and focus on practical language skills.
