        School Fee Policy #

        1. Fee Confirmation
          Parents are requested to confirm with the school during drop-off whether their school fees have been successfully processed and ensure that all references are correctly identified.

        2. Fee Structure
          A clear fee payment structure will be communicated to all parents at the start of the year. Payment deadlines will be set, and reminders sent to parents via email or SMS.

        3. Unpaid Fees
          For parents with unpaid school fees, full payment of the year’s fees will be required moving forward. This policy aims to reduce administrative tasks and allow the school to focus more on students’ education.

        4. Financial Assistance
          Families facing financial difficulties may apply for assistance or installment payment plans.

        5. Early Payment Discount
          A small discount will be offered to parents who pay fees in advance.

        This policy is effective immediately and applies to all parents and guardians.

        Absence #

        Times and Attendance Policy

        Operating Hours
        Our school operates on Sundays from 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM during term time.

        Punctuality and Attendance
        The school places great importance on punctuality and overall attendance. Students who arrive more than 10 minutes late will be issued a warning. If a student arrives late two or more times in a given week without a valid reason, a detention will be imposed.

        Absences During Term Time
        Parents/guardians must carefully consider the impact of taking their child/ren out of school during term time, as this can negatively affect the child’s education. However, we acknowledge that sometimes it may be unavoidable. In such cases, up to 3 weeks of absence in the academic year will be allowed. If a child is absent for more than three weeks without a valid reason provided in advance, the child will be removed from the register and must re-apply for re-admittance. Re-admittance is not guaranteed and will be subject to availability of places.

        Communication Regarding Absences
        All communication related to attendance and punctuality must be directed to the School Lead. Parents/guardians are required to inform the teacher via the group WhatsApp if an absence is expected. Persistent short-term lateness or absences will be treated seriously.

        Drop-Off and Collection Policy #

        Classroom Access
        For the safety and security of both the children and the school, parents/guardians are not permitted to enter any of the classrooms unless prior approval has been given in advance. This measure ensures a secure environment for all students and staff during drop-off and collection times.
