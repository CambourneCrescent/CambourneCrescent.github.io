    Curriculum and Learning Resources Policy

    1. Curriculum Review
      The Arabic and Islamic Studies curriculum will undergo an annual review to ensure alignment with current educational standards and the evolving needs of students.

    2. Resource Availability
      Teachers will be equipped with necessary learning materials to effectively deliver the curriculum.
      A small library will be established to support student learning, with a structured plan for regular additions to the collection.

    3. Parent Engagement
      At the start of each term, the curriculum and syllabus will be shared with parents.
      Guidelines will be provided to help parents support their child’s learning effectively at home.

    This policy is designed to enhance educational quality and foster collaboration between the school and parents.
